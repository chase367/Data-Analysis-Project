# Final Recommendations

Based on the funnel and revenue analysis, three areas stood out as priorities: UX, marketing spend allocation, and financial guardrails.

## 1. UX & Website Optimization

**Don't touch the checkout flow.** Conversion from Checkout Start → Purchase is excellent (~80%+), indicating the technical payment flow is frictionless.

- **Action:** Do not redesign the checkout page right now — doing so risks breaking something that's already working well. Engineering and design resources are better spent upstream in the funnel, where drop-off is actually occurring.

## 2. Marketing Strategy

**Stop over-investing in social for direct sales.** Social media drives ~30% of traffic (volume) but has the lowest conversion rate (efficiency) of any channel — indicating we're largely paying for "window shoppers" rather than buyers.

- **Action:** Shift budget away from "Traffic" objectives on social ads and toward "Retargeting" or "Lead Gen" campaigns that capture emails instead of chasing one-time site visits.

**Double down on email marketing.** Email is the highest-converting channel (~13%+ conversion vs. ~6% for social).

- **Action:** Implement an aggressive email capture popup targeted at high-volume social visitors. The data shows that once these visitors are on the email list, they're far more likely to convert later — even if they don't buy on their first visit.

## 3. Financial & Revenue

**Audit ad spend against Average Order Value.** Average Order Value (AOV) across the funnel is ~$115.

- **Action:** Set a strict Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) ceiling. If we're paying more than $30–$40 to acquire a customer through social ads — a channel that already converts poorly — those transactions are likely unprofitable once acquisition cost is factored in.

## Summary

| Area | Finding | Action |
|---|---|---|
| UX | Checkout converts at ~80%+ | Leave it alone — focus effort upstream |
| Marketing | Social = high volume, low conversion (~6%) | Shift to retargeting/lead gen |
| Marketing | Email = highest conversion (~13%+) | Invest in email capture |
| Finance | AOV ≈ $115 | Cap CAC at $30–$40 for underperforming channels |
